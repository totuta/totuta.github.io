Studying a grammar book will not teach you to speak a foreign language.
Likewise, studying scales or chord tones will not teach you to play guitar beyond chord accompaniment.

You can learn to speak a foreign language only by repeatedly saying the next word or the next meaningful chunk yourself.
A Large Language Model (LLM), too, can function only by improving its ability to predict the next token. The method used for that is iterative training with cross-entropy as the learning signal.

To play guitar beyond chord accompaniment, you have to improve your ability to play, in real time, the note or phrase that should come next. The only way is iterative trial and error.

It took me more than 30 years of playing guitar to realize this.
