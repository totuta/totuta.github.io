So why do we divide by `sqrt(d_k)` when scaling?

$$
  \mathrm{Attention}(Q, K, V) =
\mathrm{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$

Not completely statistically precise, but statistically useful enough.

For random vectors with dimensionality `d_k`, the variance of their dot product grows with `d_k`, and the standard deviation is on the order of `sqrt(d_k)`.

That is why we divide by `sqrt(d_k)` to normalize the scale.

Without that normalization:

- dot products can become extremely large
- softmax computation can become numerically unstable

Here is the torch-style code:

```ts
export function scale(dot: number, dimension: number) {
  return dot / Math.sqrt(dimension);
}
```
